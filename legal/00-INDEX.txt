IMPORTANT NOTE
--------------
This folder contains the complete legal framework of the Notes Sathi platform.
These documents are published in plain-text format so that every student and
teacher can read them on any device, without any special software. They are
written to be firm, fair and enforceable under the laws of India. In case of
any conflict between a summary shown inside the app and the full text in this
folder, THE FULL TEXT IN THIS FOLDER SHALL PREVAIL.

Nothing in this folder creates an attorney-client relationship. The documents
are standard, good-faith legal protections for a free educational community
platform and may be reviewed by a qualified advocate at any time.


DOCUMENTS IN THIS FOLDER
------------------------
00-INDEX.txt
    This file. Overview, order of precedence and official contact points.

01-TERMS-AND-CONDITIONS.txt
    The master agreement between you and the platform. Covers the nature of
    the service, eligibility, your obligations, intellectual property,
    disclaimers, limitation of liability, indemnity, governing law and
    jurisdiction.

02-PRIVACY-POLICY.txt
    Explains exactly what data the platform does and does NOT collect, what
    is stored in your own browser, how the upload token is handled, the
    public nature of the GitHub repository, and compliance with the Digital
    Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

03-COPYRIGHT-POLICY-AND-TAKEDOWN-NOTICE.txt
    The platform's copyright compliance framework: the educational fair-dealing
    position under Section 52 of the Copyright Act, 1957, the notice-and-
    takedown procedure, counter-notices, timelines and repeat-infringer policy.

04-DISCLAIMER.txt
    Clear statements that the platform is NOT an official publication of any
    college, university or examination body, that content is contributed
    "as is", and that trademarks belong to their respective owners.

05-ACCEPTABLE-USE-POLICY.txt
    What may and may not be uploaded, shared or done on the platform, and the
    consequences of violation.

06-GRIEVANCE-REDRESSAL-AND-FIRST-COMPLAINT-REPORT.txt
    The official Grievance Redressal Mechanism, including the FIRST COMPLAINT
    REPORT form (a ready-to-fill template for reporting any problem —
    copyright, privacy, defamation, error or safety — for the first time),
    response timelines and escalation paths.

07-CONTENT-CONTRIBUTION-LICENSE.txt
    The licence granted by every teacher / contributor to the platform when
    they upload notes, together with the warranties they give about ownership
    of the uploaded material.
