1. THE SHORT VERSION (PLAIN SUMMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READING NOTES COLLECTS NOTHING. There are:
  * NO account needed to read — just open and study;
  * NO advertisements;
  * NO analytics or tracking of any kind;
  * NO cookies that follow you around;
  * NO sale, sharing or transfer of your data to anyone — ever.
Everything the app remembers about you as a reader (your theme, your branch,
your semester, your bookmarks) is saved ONLY in your own browser, on your
own device, and never leaves it.

OPTIONAL STUDENT ACCOUNTS (introduced October 2026): if you CHOOSE to create
an account for read-ticks and reactions, we store exactly four things —
your display name, e-mail, phone number and your reactions — with Google
Firebase. Nothing else. Passwords are hashed by Google and can never be
seen or recovered by anyone, including us. You can request deletion of your
account data anytime via vinay.sanjaysoni@gmail.com.

This policy explains the details, and is made in compliance with the Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

2. DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  "Personal Data" has the meaning given in the Digital Personal Data
     Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").
2.2  "Local Storage" means the browser technology (window.localStorage) used
     by the app to save preferences on your device.
2.3  "Contributor" means a teacher / person who publishes notes using the
     Upload Dashboard.
2.4  "Student Account" means the optional account a student may create to
     use read-ticks (✅) and reactions (❤️). Reading notes never requires
     an account.

3. WHAT WE COLLECT (AND WHAT WE DON'T)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  READERS (no account): the Platform does not collect, store, process,
     transmit, share, sell or otherwise handle ANY Personal Data. It does
     NOT operate:
     (a)  any user account system;
     (b)  any analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or fingerprinting service;
     (c)  any advertising or ad-network SDK;
     (d)  any third-party tracker, pixel or tag;
     (e)  any server or database of its own — the entire site is static
          hosting on GitHub Pages.

3.2  Because no Personal Data is collected from readers, no consent is
     required under the DPDP Act for the mere reading of notes.

3.3  STUDENT ACCOUNTS (optional, opt-in only): creating an account stores
     exactly the following with Google Firebase (Spark plan):
     (a)  Display name — shown beside your reactions and on your two-letter
          avatar (initials);
     (b)  E-mail address — your login ID and password-reset channel;
     (c)  Phone number — collected for identity verification during the
          manual approval process; NEVER displayed publicly, visible only
          to the Grievance Officer (admin);
     (d)  Reactions — which notes you read (✅) or marked helpful (❤️),
          with time-stamps.
     Your PASSWORD is stored only as a cryptographic (scrypt) hash by
     Google Firebase Authentication — it is never visible to the Platform,
     never transmitted to us, and cannot be recovered by anyone.

3.4  WHERE IT IS PROCESSED: account data is stored on Google Firebase
     servers (project: notes-sathi-ae2e5). Google LLC acts as the
     infrastructure/processor; the Platform is the data fiduciary.
     No other third party receives any account data.

3.5  PURPOSE & MINIMISATION: account data is used solely to (a) operate
     read-ticks and reactions, (b) verify students during the manual
     approval process, and (c) moderate the community (rename or remove
     abusive names, remove reactions). It is NOT used for marketing,
     profiling or any automated decision-making, and is never sold or
     shared.

4. WHAT IS STORED, AND WHERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  The app uses Local Storage in YOUR browser only. At any time you can
     erase everything by clearing your browser's site data. The items are:
     (a)  Theme preference (light / dark mode);
     (b)  Your chosen Program, Branch and Semester (e.g. Polytechnic /
          Civil / Semester 3);
     (c)  Bookmarked (saved) notes — a list of note identifiers;
     (d)  "Seen" markers that power the NEW badge and story rings;
     (e)  Acknowledgment that you accepted the terms;
     (f)  For Contributors ONLY: the "remembered" GitHub sign-in state
          described in Clause 6;
     (g)  For signed-in students ONLY: a Firebase login session token, so
          you stay signed in.
     None of the reader items (a)-(e) contain your name, phone number,
     e-mail, or any identifier that can identify you as a natural person.

4.2  This data never leaves your device. The app contains no code that
     transmits Local Storage contents anywhere.

5. PUBLIC NATURE OF THE REPOSITORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  The notes themselves live in a PUBLIC GitHub repository. Everything a
     Contributor uploads is, by design, publicly visible at
     https://github.com/VinaySoni-IN/Notes-Sathi, including the file name,
     file size, upload date and the public GitHub identity of the account
     that uploaded it. This is an inherent, disclosed feature of the
     Platform — Contributors should upload only material they are happy to
     host publicly.

5.2  GitHub, as the host, may process standard server logs (IP addresses,
     request times) under its own privacy policy available at
     https://github.com/site/privacy. The Owner has no access to, and no
     control over, GitHub's logs.

6. CONTRIBUTOR TOKENS (UPLOAD DASHBOARD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  A Contributor signs in with a GitHub fine-grained personal access token
     ("Token") that the Contributor creates personally on github.com.

6.2  If "Remember on this device" is ticked, the Token is stored in the
     Contributor's own browser's Local Storage; otherwise it is kept only in
     memory for the session. The Token is transmitted only to GitHub's
     official API (api.github.com) over HTTPS, and only from the
     Contributor's own device.

6.3  The Owner NEVER receives, views, stores or logs any Token. Contributors
     are advised to use a fine-grained Token limited to this repository with
     Contents read/write permission only, and to sign out on shared
     computers.

7. CHILDREN AND STUDENTS WHO ARE MINORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  READING: many users are students under 18. Reading collects no data
     whatsoever, so no child's data is processed while simply studying.

7.2  STUDENT ACCOUNTS: under the DPDP Act, 2023, processing the personal
     data of a child (under 18) requires verifiable parental consent.
     Therefore, students under 18 who wish to create a Student Account
     must have a parent or guardian send a short consent message (with the
     student's name) to vinay.sanjaysoni@gmail.com or WhatsApp
     +91 89890 31351 BEFORE the account is approved. Accounts without
     such consent on file for a minor remain unapproved.

7.3  Parents or guardians who become aware of any material concerning a
     child may raise it through the Grievance channels in Clause 10; such
     reports receive the highest priority.

8. YOUR RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Under the DPDP Act, data principals have rights of access,
     correction, erasure, nomination and grievance redressal.

     READERS (no account): we hold nothing — everything is already in your
     hands:
     (a)  To "erase" all your data: clear the site's data in your browser
          settings, or use your browser's private/incognito mode;
     (b)  To withdraw the terms acknowledgment: clear site data.

     STUDENT ACCOUNTS: write to vinay.sanjaysoni@gmail.com (or any channel
     in Clause 10) and we will:
     (c)  ACCESS — tell you what data your account holds;
     (d)  CORRECT — fix your name, e-mail or phone number;
     (e)  ERASE — delete your account profile and all your reactions
          (processed within 15 days; Firebase may retain a deleted-account
          record for a short period as per its own policy);
     (f)  WITHDRAW CONSENT — you may delete your account or simply sign
          out; read-ticks and reactions stop immediately. Withdrawing
          consent does not affect reading — that always stays free and
          account-free.

8.2  Contributors who wish to have their uploaded content (and the public
     record of their GitHub identity next to it) removed may request removal
     under the takedown and grievance procedures in
     legal/03-COPYRIGHT-POLICY-AND-TAKEDOWN-NOTICE.txt and
     legal/06-GRIEVANCE-REDRESSAL-AND-FIRST-COMPLAINT-REPORT.txt. Note that
     prior git history on GitHub may retain older commits; the Owner will
     use GitHub's support channels where complete history erasure is
     genuinely required.
