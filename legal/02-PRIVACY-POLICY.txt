1. THE SHORT VERSION (PLAIN SUMMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Notes Sathi collects NO personal data. There are:
  * NO accounts and NO sign-ups for reading;
  * NO advertisements;
  * NO analytics or tracking of any kind;
  * NO cookies that follow you around;
  * NO sale, sharing or transfer of your data to anyone — ever.
Everything the app remembers about you (your theme, your branch, your
semester, your bookmarks) is saved ONLY in your own browser, on your own
device, and never leaves it.

This policy explains the details, and is made in compliance with the Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

2. DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  "Personal Data" has the meaning given in the Digital Personal Data
     Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").
2.2  "Local Storage" means the browser technology (window.localStorage) used
     by the app to save preferences on your device.
2.3  "Contributor" means a teacher / person who publishes notes using the
     Upload Dashboard.

3. WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  The Platform does not collect, store, process, transmit, share, sell or
     otherwise handle ANY Personal Data of its readers. Specifically, the
     Platform does NOT operate:
     (a)  any user account system;
     (b)  any analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or fingerprinting service;
     (c)  any advertising or ad-network SDK;
     (d)  any third-party tracker, pixel or tag;
     (e)  any server or database of its own — the entire site is static
          hosting on GitHub Pages.

3.2  Because no Personal Data is collected, no consent is required from any
     User under the DPDP Act for the mere reading of notes.

4. WHAT IS STORED, AND WHERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  The app uses Local Storage in YOUR browser only. At any time you can
     erase everything by clearing your browser's site data. The items are:
     (a)  Theme preference (light / dark mode);
     (b)  Your chosen Program, Branch and Semester (e.g. Polytechnic /
          Civil / Semester 3);
     (c)  Bookmarked (saved) notes — a list of note identifiers;
     (d)  "Seen" markers that power the NEW badge and story rings;
     (e)  Acknowledgment that you accepted the terms;
     (f)  For Contributors ONLY: the "remembered" GitHub sign-in state
          described in Clause 6.
     None of these items contain your name, phone number, e-mail, or any
     identifier that can identify you as a natural person.

4.2  This data never leaves your device. The app contains no code that
     transmits Local Storage contents anywhere.

5. PUBLIC NATURE OF THE REPOSITORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  The notes themselves live in a PUBLIC GitHub repository. Everything a
     Contributor uploads is, by design, publicly visible at
     https://github.com/VinaySoni-IN/Notes-Sathi, including the file name,
     file size, upload date and the public GitHub identity of the account
     that uploaded it. This is an inherent, disclosed feature of the
     Platform — Contributors should upload only material they are happy to
     host publicly.

5.2  GitHub, as the host, may process standard server logs (IP addresses,
     request times) under its own privacy policy available at
     https://github.com/site/privacy. The Owner has no access to, and no
     control over, GitHub's logs.

6. CONTRIBUTOR TOKENS (UPLOAD DASHBOARD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  A Contributor signs in with a GitHub fine-grained personal access token
     ("Token") that the Contributor creates personally on github.com.

6.2  If "Remember on this device" is ticked, the Token is stored in the
     Contributor's own browser's Local Storage; otherwise it is kept only in
     memory for the session. The Token is transmitted only to GitHub's
     official API (api.github.com) over HTTPS, and only from the
     Contributor's own device.

6.3  The Owner NEVER receives, views, stores or logs any Token. Contributors
     are advised to use a fine-grained Token limited to this repository with
     Contents read/write permission only, and to sign out on shared
     computers.

7. CHILDREN AND STUDENTS WHO ARE MINORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Many users of the Platform are students who may be under 18. The
     Platform does not knowingly collect any Personal Data from anyone,
     including children, and therefore processes no children's data
     whatsoever.

7.2  Parents or guardians who become aware of any material concerning a
     child may raise it through the Grievance channels in Clause 10; such
     reports receive the highest priority.

8. YOUR RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Under the DPDP Act, data principals have rights such as access,
     correction, erasure and grievance redressal. Since the Platform holds
     no Personal Data of readers, there is nothing to access, correct or
     erase on our side — everything is already in your hands:
     (a)  To "erase" all your data: clear the site's data in your browser
          settings, or use your browser's private/incognito mode;
     (b)  To withdraw the terms acknowledgment: clear site data;
     (c)  For anything else, write to the Grievance Officer (Clause 10).

8.2  Contributors who wish to have their uploaded content (and the public
     record of their GitHub identity next to it) removed may request removal
     under the takedown and grievance procedures in
     legal/03-COPYRIGHT-POLICY-AND-TAKEDOWN-NOTICE.txt and
     legal/06-GRIEVANCE-REDRESSAL-AND-FIRST-COMPLAINT-REPORT.txt. Note that
     prior git history on GitHub may retain older commits; the Owner will
     use GitHub's support channels where complete history erasure is
     genuinely required.
