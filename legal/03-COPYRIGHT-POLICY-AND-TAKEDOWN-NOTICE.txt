1. OUR POSITION ON COPYRIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  The Platform respects intellectual property rights. The purpose of
     Notes Sathi is genuinely educational: free, non-commercial sharing of
     teacher-created study material with students.

1.2  The Platform is an INTERMEDIARY under Section 2(1)(w) of the
     Information Technology Act, 2000. Except for material uploaded by the
     Owner himself, the Platform does not create or select Content; it only
     provides the technical means of publication. The Platform therefore
     claims the safe-harbour protection of Section 79 of the said Act,
     having complied with the due-diligence requirements of the Information
     Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code)
     Rules, 2021, including the notice-based takedown mechanism below.

1.3  Nothing in this policy limits the rights of any copyright owner.

2. THE EDUCATIONAL CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Section 52(1)(i) of the Copyright Act, 1957 permits the reproduction of
     any work by a teacher or a pupil in the course of instruction. A
     substantial part of the Platform's Content consists of material shared
     by teachers with their own pupils for the course of instruction.

2.2  Section 52(1)(a) of the Copyright Act, 1957 also permits fair dealing
     with any work for the purposes of private or personal use, including
     research, and for criticism or review.

2.3  The Platform is completely free of charge, carries no advertising and
     earns no revenue of any kind — facts consistent with the
     educational-fair-dealing position.

2.4  Nevertheless, if any Content on the Platform infringes copyright, the
     Owner will remove it promptly upon a valid notice, without requiring
     the copyright owner to first obtain a court order.

3. WHAT CONTRIBUTORS PROMISE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Every Contributor, by uploading, warrants that they own the rights to
     the material or are otherwise permitted (by the author, co-authors or
     licence) to publish it, as set out in
     legal/07-CONTENT-CONTRIBUTION-LICENSE.txt.

3.2  Contributors must NOT upload scanned or copied commercial textbooks,
     coaching-institute materials, paid question banks or any other
     third-party material they do not control. See the Acceptable Use
     Policy (legal/05-ACCEPTABLE-USE-POLICY.txt).

4. HOW TO FILE A COPYRIGHT / TAKEDOWN NOTICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  If you believe any Content on the Platform infringes your copyright or
     that of a person you are authorised to represent, send a written
     notice containing ALL of the following:

     (a)  Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
          (title, author/owner, and where the original is published, if
          applicable);
     (b)  Identification of the infringing Content on the Platform — the
          exact file name and its path (e.g. "notes/Polytechnic/Civil
          Engineering/Sem 3/Building Construction/example.pdf") or the
          exact page/screen where it appears;
     (c)  Your full name, address, telephone number and e-mail;
     (d)  A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the
          Content is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or
          the law;
     (e)  A statement, under penalty of perjury / misrepresentation, that
          the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the
          copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf;
     (f)  Your physical or electronic signature (a typed full legal name
          suffices for electronic notices).

4.2  Send the notice through ANY of these official channels:

     WhatsApp : +91 89890 31351 (Grievance Officer: Vinay Soni)
     E-mail   : vinay.sanjaysoni@gmail.com
              (subject line: "Copyright Notice — <file name>")
     Instagram: @vinay.sanjay.soni
     Written  : https://github.com/VinaySoni-IN/Notes-Sathi/issues
                (a GitHub Issue titled "Copyright Notice — <file name>"
                creates a permanent, time-stamped record — recommended)

4.3  RESPONSE TIMELINES:
     (a)  Acknowledgment      : within 48 hours of receipt;
     (b)  Interim action      : the impugned Content is disabled or removed
                                from the Platform within 72 hours of a
                                facially valid notice, pending verification;
     (c)  Final decision      : a reasoned decision within 7 days; if the
                                notice is found invalid (e.g. the use is
                                protected fair dealing), a written
                                explanation will be provided;
     (d)  Grievance closure   : within 15 days in accordance with the
                                Grievance Redressal Mechanism
                                (legal/06-GRIEVANCE-REDRESSAL-AND-FIRST-
                                COMPLAINT-REPORT.txt).

5. COUNTER-NOTICE BY THE CONTRIBUTOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  A Contributor whose Content was removed may submit a counter-notice
     with the same particulars as Clause 4.1, stating under penalty of
     perjury that the removal was a mistake or misidentification, and
     consenting to the jurisdiction of the courts at Balaghat, Madhya
     Pradesh.

5.2  The Platform will restore the Content within 10 (ten) business days of
     the counter-notice unless it receives a copy of a court / police
     complaint or order obtained by the original complainant within that
     period, or unless the Content is independently found unlawful.

6. REPEAT INFRINGERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Contributors who are the subject of two or more upheld infringement
     notices will be permanently removed from the list of "Contributed
     Teachers" and their future contributions declined.
